Hi! My name is Tibet, I am a second year student at UC Berkeley aiming to double major in data science and philosophy. My interests include and are not limited to philosophy, algorithms, classics, data structures, machine learning, chess, and music. At Berkeley, I am the Co-President of DataGood, a club that combines data science for social good. I also love all things music, which is why I founded Berkeley EDMA, Berkeley's very own Electronic Dance Music Association. I grew up playing the piano and violin, but at Berkeley I've been able to explore electronic music production and DJing. Another hobby of mine is photography, which you can view on this website! A few fun facts about me: I have an almost perfect understanding of pitch, I am very quickly adaptable to new environments, and I built this entire website with prompt engineering :o.

Feel free to explore the other sections of my site to discover my music, photography, and professional achievements!
